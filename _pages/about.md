<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Information Science and Technology, <a class="intro-link" href="https://ustc.edu.cn/">University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)</a>. I am affiliated with the <a class="intro-link" href="https://leinao.ustc.edu.cn/main.htm">National Engineering Laboratory for Brain-inspired Intelligence Technology and Applications</a> and advised by Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.ustc.edu.cn/xuejinchen/zh_CN/index.htm">Xuejin Chen</a>. My research training also includes an internship and joint training with Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/wm12/zh_CN/index.htm">Meng Wang (IEEE/IAPR Fellow)</a>'s group at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Hefei Comprehensive National Science Center, with guidance from Assoc. Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://happyisac.github.io/PersonHomePage/">Jinyang Huang</a> and Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/gd/zh_CN/index.htm">Dan Guo</a>. I received my bachelor's degree in Microelectronics from <a class="intro-link" href="https://www.hfut.edu.cn/">Hefei University of Technology (HFUT)</a> in 2024.

<p class='research-intro' id='research-intro'>My research focuses on developing agents with spatial understanding and affective cognition that can adapt their interactions to environmental changes and individual differences. I explore how to integrate 3D spatial reasoning with the modeling of emotion, personality, and cognitive states from visual and physiological signals, while investigating how the study of neural structure and function can inform the design of perceptual and cognitive mechanisms in these agents.</p>

My work has appeared in international journals and conferences, including Nature Communications, PNAS Nexus, ACM MM/<wbr>UbiComp/<wbr>TAAS, and IEEE TMM/<wbr>TIFS/<wbr>TDSC/<wbr>TAFFC/<wbr>TCE/<wbr>TGRS/<wbr>ICME/<wbr>ICASSP {% include scholar_citation_badge.html %}. I have received the National Scholarship for Graduate Students, the Climbing Star Scholarship, and other awards.
