# 个人简介

-->

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Information Science and Technology, <a class="intro-link" href="https://en.ustc.edu.cn/">University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)</a>, affiliated with the <a class="intro-link" href="https://leinao.ustc.edu.cn/main.htm">National Engineering Laboratory for Brain-inspired Intelligence Technology and Application</a> and advised by Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.ustc.edu.cn/xuejinchen/zh_CN/index.htm">Xuejin Chen</a>. During my graduate training, I joint training with Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/wm12/zh_CN/index.htm">Meng Wang (IEEE/IAPR Fellow)</a>'s group at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Hefei Comprehensive National Science Center, advised by Assoc. Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://happyisac.github.io/PersonHomePage/">Jinyang Huang</a> and Prof. <a class="intro-link" href="https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/gd/zh_CN/index.htm">Dan Guo</a>. I received my B.Eng. in Microelectronics from <a class="intro-link" href="https://www.hfut.edu.cn/">Hefei University of Technology</a> in 2024.

My research focuses on multimodal representation learning and intelligent human-computer interaction. I study representations with spatiotemporal structure, cross-modal consistency, and cross-subject generalization from visual, neurophysiological, and environmental sensing data, enabling reliable understanding of human and system states and intelligent interaction in brain-computer interfaces, affective computing, and embodied intelligence. My work {% include scholar_citation_badge.html %} has appeared in Nature Communications, PNAS Nexus, ACM MM/<wbr>UbiComp/<wbr>TAAS, and IEEE TMM/<wbr>TIFS/<wbr>TDSC/<wbr>TAFFC/<wbr>TCE/<wbr>TGRS/<wbr>ICME/<wbr>ICASSP. My honors include the National Scholarship for Graduate Students and the Pandeng Star Scholarship.
